## Client-facing proposal language

Do not expose internal design, development, proposal, mockup, migration, source-page, or implementation language in user-facing UI copy. Client-facing pages must read as final marketing or operational content, even during proposal/mockup work. Avoid phrases such as "current site source", "based on the current page", "proposal layout", "mockup", "placeholder", "reorganized", "pulled from", or anything that reveals how the page was assembled.
